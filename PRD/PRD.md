# StudentHub Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Overview

StudentHub is a centralized platform designed to help college students discover and register for opportunities such as hackathons, workshops, internships, competitions, placement drives, and club events.

The platform aims to eliminate fragmented communication channels and provide a single destination for opportunity discovery.

---

# Problem Statement

Students currently rely on multiple sources such as WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, Instagram pages, college notice boards, and peer networks to discover opportunities.

Due to this fragmented system:

* Students miss deadlines.
* Event participation decreases.
* Important opportunities remain undiscovered.
* Students spend excessive time searching for relevant events.

---

# Product Goal

Provide students with a centralized platform where they can easily discover, search, and register for opportunities.

---

# Target Audience

## Primary Users

College students aged 18-25.

### Characteristics

* Looking for internships.
* Interested in hackathons.
* Interested in workshops.
* Building resumes and skills.

---

## Secondary Users

Club coordinators and event organizers.

### Characteristics

* Need event visibility.
* Need higher participation.
* Need simplified promotion channels.

---

# Success Metrics

## Activation Rate

Percentage of users viewing at least three opportunities during their first session.

Target: 70%

---

## Weekly Active Users

Target: 500+

---

## Event Registration Conversion

Percentage of event page visitors who register.

Target: 20%

---

## User Retention

Percentage of users returning within seven days.

Target: 40%

---

# Core Features

## Event Feed

Students can browse all available opportunities.

### Benefits

* Easy discovery.
* Centralized access.

---

## Search Functionality

Students can search opportunities by keyword.

### Benefits

* Faster navigation.
* Improved user experience.

---

## Event Details

Students can view:

* Description
* Date
* Venue
* Organizer
* Registration link

---

## Event Registration

Students can register directly through the platform.

---

# MVP Scope

Included:

* Login
* Event Feed
* Search
* Event Details
* Registration

Excluded:

* AI Recommendations
* Notifications
* Chat Features
* Social Features

---

# User Stories

## Story 1

As a student, I want to browse opportunities so that I can discover relevant events.

---

## Story 2

As a student, I want to search opportunities so that I can quickly find events matching my interests.

---

## Story 3

As a student, I want to view event details so that I can decide whether to participate.

---

## Story 4

As a student, I want to register for events so that I can participate easily.

---

# Risks

* Low user adoption.
* Incomplete event database.
* Poor event quality.

# Future Enhancements

StudentHub addresses the challenge of fragmented opportunity discovery by providing students with a centralized platform for finding and registering for relevant academic and career opportunities.
