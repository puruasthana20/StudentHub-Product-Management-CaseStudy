# StudentHub | Product Management Case Study

## Overview

StudentHub is a Product Management case study focused on solving the problem of fragmented opportunity discovery among college students.

The project was created to understand how students discover internships, hackathons, workshops, competitions, and placement opportunities, identify pain points in the current process, and define an MVP solution using standard product management practices.

---

## Problem Statement

College students often miss valuable opportunities because information is distributed across multiple channels such as WhatsApp groups, Instagram pages, Telegram channels, and peer networks.

As a result:

* Students miss registration deadlines.
* Important opportunities remain undiscovered.
* Information is often incomplete or difficult to access.

---

## User Research

A survey was conducted among college students to validate the problem and understand user behavior.

### Key Findings

* **100%** of respondents reported missing opportunities because they were unaware of them.
* **80%** of respondents were open to using a centralized opportunity discovery platform (Yes + Maybe).
* The most common frustration was receiving incomplete information about opportunities.
* The primary discovery channels were Friends (40%), Instagram (30%), WhatsApp (20%), and Telegram (10%).

## Proposed Solution

StudentHub is envisioned as a centralized platform where students can:

* Discover opportunities
* Search opportunities by category
* View complete event details
* Register for opportunities
* Track saved opportunities
